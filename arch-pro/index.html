Arch PRO · Product page

Three prototypes,
one decision.

Working prototypes of the Arch PRO product page exploring how a multi-configurable contract stand should be presented. All three are clickable and live in the browser, no design tool required. The latest version applies Pedestal's design tokens (typography, colour, spacing) and includes the below-fold sections.

Matches the existing Pedestal Rover and Power Bar pages — image row across the top, compact 4-column configurator strip at the bottom, side-panel cart drawer that doesn't block the page so the 3D model keeps updating live as you reconfigure. Points to take to the room

  • Bottom-strip vs. drawer-driven configuration — which fits Pedestal's voice and existing patterns better?
  • 15 SKUs at launch (3 frame × 4 cover + 3 whiteboard + 3 naked) — does a configurator buy us anything beyond visual fidelity?
  • "No cover" as a 4th cover option, plus upsells only when Naked is selected — sensible product logic?
  • Pro Resources content offer instead of an RFQ form — agreed direction for B2B lead-gen?
  • Building model-viewer + Blender in-house instead of subscribing to Fibble — confirm with engineering?
  • Audience scoping inside Pedestal Studio — are we comfortable extending Studio for contract products at all?
